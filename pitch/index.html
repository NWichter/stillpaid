03 / 13 · Insight

The rule already exists.
Just not without a middleman.

Deemed acceptance · § 640 (2) BGB, contracts for workSilence past a deadline you set counts as acceptance, unless the client names a defect.

But nothing pays out automatically. The money still sits with the client, and the freelancer still has to chase it, or sue.

MarketplacesUpwork and Fiverr auto-release after 3–14 days.

But only inside their marketplace, for 10–20 %, with them holding the money. Not for clients you found yourself.

Stillpaid brings optimistic payments to client work: final unless the client objects in time. The money is locked before work starts, and silence releases it.

04 / 13 · How it works

Milestone escrow, with a clock.

1 · FundMoney first

The client funds each milestone in USDC. Held by the program in that milestone's own vault, not by us.

2 · SubmitDeliver

Link and note. Their SHA-256 fingerprint goes on-chain, so any later edit shows.

3 · ReviewSilent Yes

Approve, send a change request, or dispute. Silence past the review deadline: the freelancer is paid.

4 · Disagree?Split, arbiter, default

An agreed split, a named arbiter, or the default split both sides accepted up front.

By design: every state has a timed exitstillpaid.sorevo.de

06 / 13 · Disputes

Clients keep the full review.
They just can't ghost.

1 · NegotiateOffer a split

One side offers e.g. 60/40; the other accepts exactly that offer. A changed offer can't be accepted by accident.

2 · ArbiterA person both trust

Optional, named when the job is created. Decides only after the negotiation window.

3 · Default splitAgreed up front

Nobody decided in time? The default split from the terms applies. Anyone can trigger it.

Client: money back before acceptance or on late delivery
Client: the agreed number of change requests
Freelancer: sees the money locked before starting
Freelancer: a change request never shortens a deadline

08 / 13 · Why Solana

Trust the rules, not a custodian.

01Nobody holds the money

Held by the program, not by us. Every payout follows the rules and happens once. Before mainnet, upgrades move to a public multisig.

02Deadlines enforce themselves

Release on silence and the default split are permissionless: any wallet can trigger them, on Solana's clock.

03Paid anywhere in seconds

USDC settles on-chain in seconds for a fraction of a cent. The app pays the fees, so users need no SOL.

04Evidence both sides can check

Terms, deliveries and reasons are fingerprinted on-chain. The job page re-hashes every text and links every step.
